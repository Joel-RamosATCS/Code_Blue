    LEARN

    Reading the room.

    Vital signs are observations that help build a picture of a fictional case. Their meaning varies with age, health, context, and many other circumstances.

    Heart rate

    How many times the heart beats in a minute.

    Oxygen saturation

    An estimate of oxygen carried in the blood.

    Blood pressure

    The force of blood against artery walls, shown as two numbers.

    Temperature

    A measure of body heat.

    Respiratory rate

    How many breaths occur in a minute.

    ABOUT

    Designed for thoughtful practice.

    CODE BLUE uses hand-written, fictional patient information only. It is an educational game, not a clinical tool: it does not diagnose conditions, provide treatment guidance, or replace professional medical judgment.

    During supported demonstrations, interactive explanations may be generated using software running directly on the demonstration computer. The simulation works completely without this optional feature.
